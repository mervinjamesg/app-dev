True Beauty
A K-Drama About Self-Acceptance
True Beauty is a story about Lim Ju-kyung, a high school girl who masters the art of makeup to hide her bare face.

“People think I’m pretty, but it’s all just makeup.” — Ju-kyung

Han Seo-jun – The cool rebel with a soft heart
